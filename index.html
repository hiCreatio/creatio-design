Two common gaps in projects

Gap 01 Ideation → product decision

Building the wrong thing

The initial idea has not been translated into a product flow. Business, design and dev each read it differently, and the team builds in the wrong direction before there is a chance to verify it.

Gap 02 Product decision → ready for devs

Fragmented hand-off

Requirements reach dev in pieces, over chat and screenshots, with no single source of truth. Edge cases surface during coding and lead to rework; the later they are found, the more each fix costs.

Our solution

Getting business, design and dev to execute in one direction.

Business-oriented

Design decisions tied to the project’s business goals.

Data-driven

Grounded in data, so judgments have a basis.

Fluency

We speak business, product and dev, which reduces misalignment and rework between teams.

Process: from discovery to hand-off

Depending on the project, the process and the artifacts it produces can be lean or complete.

  1. Discovery

    Pin down the business problem and the critical parts before going into detail.

    OutputProblem framing + research questions

  2. Research

    Verify with company data and real users, not assumptions.

    OutputData synthesis + insights

  3. User / task flow

    Map user and system flows to surface bottlenecks early.

    OutputUser flow + task flow diagrams

  4. IA

    Structure information the way users look for it.

    OutputIA diagram / sitemap

  5. Wireframe

    Lock the structure and content of every screen.

    OutputWireframes for every screen

  6. Hi-fi

    Detailed UI, every state, on brand.

    OutputHi-fi UI with all states, interactive prototype

  7. Design spec

    A spec engineers can measure: dimensions, behavior, edge cases.

    OutputDev-ready design spec for engineers

What clients say

Engagement model and packages

Design Audit

Context & goal

Complex UX flows with logic gaps before development. A review to improve them or reduce the risk of rework.

Scope & output

  • UX & system flow audit
  • UX audit & logic gaps report
  • P0 to P3 prioritized backlog

Time & capacity

One week minimum, longer with the product’s size and complexity.

Operations & exit

  • Two alignment sessions
  • Hard exit: a clean sign-off, free to stop or continue

Scope-based

Context & goal

Work by scope with a fixed timeline while dev waits on specs. Closes out a complex workstream completely.

Scope & output

  • One or more modules of your choice (flow, UI, DS)
  • Figma design spec
  • Full states, logic & edge cases

Time & capacity

Set by scope and delivery milestones, agreed on the call.

Operations & exit

  • Reviews at each milestone
  • Sign-off against the spec committed at the start

Retainer

Context & goal

Long-term product, a continuous backlog, a gap in in-depth design capability.

Scope & output

  • Owns UX/UI against the product backlog
  • Direct engineering alignment & design QA
  • Delivery directly in your workspace

Time & capacity

A weekly block of hours at the level you commit to (for example 15 or 25 hours).

Operations & exit

  • Runs on your team’s sprint cadence
  • Unlimited revisions within the hours
  • Adjust or pause on a flexible cycle
