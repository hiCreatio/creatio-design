AI workflow

AI compresses my hours. The judgment stays mine.

I use AI daily in core design work, not as a novelty. The honest version of what that means:

01 · What it accelerates

Research and domain ramp-up

Learning a new domain used to cost me two months. With a structured research pipeline it takes one to two weeks, with every claim tied to a source I can show.

02 · What it opens

Prototypes in code, not pictures

Concepts go from Figma to working React prototypes your team can click, test on a phone, and react to. Feedback lands on behavior, not on a static image.

03 · What stays mine

The diagnosis and the call

AI produces artifacts. It doesn't survive being questioned about a decision it never made. The trade-offs, the priorities, and the "no" are mine to defend.

Where I've rejected AI output, concretely: on a recent market assessment, the research pipeline surfaced a regulatory claim that made the recommendation easy. A verification pass showed the legislation never passed. The convenient story was discarded and the recommendation was rebuilt on what was actually true. That verification step is part of the workflow, not an afterthought.
